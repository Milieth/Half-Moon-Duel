Goal: Allow Player to play Half moon game As server or client
Requirement:
. player can't move if this is not their turn
. no change to game layout
. update both state every move

Network Flow:
. set initial board state(server/client)
. request to move a card(server/client)
. validation card move(server)
. pattern matching and point logic(server)
. broadcast update board state(server)
. end player turn(server)
. . switch player turn
. . sync game state
. end game phase(server)
. . bonus poin phase
. . determine winner
. . end game

Network Task Structure:
. set Initial board state
. . get server or client
. . init layout and set game turn
. server send rpc draw card to all peer
. request to move a card(client)
. . prevent card to be pressed
. . send card index, node id, and sender to server
. validation card move(server)
. . check if sender match active turn
. . check if card actually exist
. . check if target node is empty
. broadcast place card event
. . place card and determine event(server)
. . send to all peer, place a card func and card event data and run it
. . . detect card event(server)
. . . convert card event to rpc packed and send it(server)
. . . receive packed and unpacking(all)
. . . implement card match event(all)
. . switch player turn to next player turn
. Get bonus poin
. . count each player bonus point(server)
. . implement bonus poin (all)
. End game
. . stop any move(all)
. . determine winner(server)
. . display winner(all)
. . display end game button(all)
