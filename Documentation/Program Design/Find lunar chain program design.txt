Goal: Find lunar chain of newly placed card
Requirement:
. lunar cycle(sequence of phase) should be 3 cards or more


Task Structure:
. init
. . get board
. . get target node
. depth first search
. . ensure target node has card in it
. . mark target node as visited node
. . . get visited node set and add target node
. . find every node that inside adjacent list (neighbors node)
. . for each neighbor inside neighbors
. . . check if neighbor marked visited
. . . . if true dont check its branch
. . . determine expected phase(-1 or +1)
. . . check neighbor match the expected phase
. . . find the neighbor branch (call depth first search again recursively)
. . . . unmark this node visited id before returning the function
. . . . return single node if neighbor don't have branch
. . . . return branch node of this neighbor
. . . . concatenate each branch node with neighbor node to construct node chain (by doing the function recursively)
. . . save each branch of neighbor node
. . . . check if this is the first branch
. . . . . true, save it
. . . . . false, save it new branch
. get lunar phase sequence of target node
. . get lunar sequence from backward path
. . . get backward path of target node
. . . for each backward path determine forward path
. . . combine backward and forward path into lunar phase sequence
. . get lunar sequence from forward path
. . . get forward path of target node
. . . for each forward path determine its backward path
. . . combine forward and backward path into lunar phase sequence
